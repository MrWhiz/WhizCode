## License Details

**Open Source:** ✅ Yes
**Community Contributions:** ✅ Required
**Commercial Use:** ❌ No (Commons Clause)
**Rebranding:** ❌ Not allowed
**Proprietary Forks:** ❌ Not allowed

### Commons Clause

**Adds one rule:** "You cannot Sell the Software"

This means:
- ❌ Cannot sell WhizCode
- ❌ Cannot offer as paid service
- ❌ Cannot charge for hosting/support
- ✅ Can use for free
- ✅ Can distribute for free
- ✅ Can use internally

### Trademark Protection

**Protected:**
- Name: "WhizCode"
- Logo: WhizCode branding
- Confusingly similar names

**Allowed:**
- "MyEditor (based on WhizCode)"
- "WhizCode Fork - [Your Modifications]"
- "WhizCode with [Feature] extension"

**Not Allowed:**
- "WhizCode Pro"
- "WhizCode Enterprise"
- Rebranding as your own product

## Why AGPL-3.0 + Commons Clause + Trademark?

**AGPL-3.0 ensures:**
- Code stays open source
- Improvements are shared
- Community benefits
- No proprietary forks

**Commons Clause ensures:**
- Cannot be sold
- Cannot be monetized
- Remains free forever
- Protects your work

**Trademark clause ensures:**
- WhizCode identity is protected
- Cannot be rebranded
- Cannot be claimed as someone else's product
- Official WhizCode remains recognizable

## Publishing

When publishing to GitHub/GitLab:

1. Include LICENSE file
2. Add license badge to README:
   ```markdown
   [![License: AGPL v3](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-AGPL%20v3-blue.svg)](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0)
   [![License: Commons Clause](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-Commons%20Clause-red.svg)](https://commonsclause.com/)
   ```

3. Add to package.json:
   ```json
   "license": "AGPL-3.0-or-later WITH Commons-Clause"
   ```

4. Add CONTRIBUTING.md with:
   - How to contribute
   - License agreement for contributors
   - Code of conduct
